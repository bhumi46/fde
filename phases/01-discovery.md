# Phase 1: Discovery

**Goal:** Understand the real problem before writing code. FDEs who skip this build the wrong thing fast.

## Tasks
1. Read `customer/brief.md` and `customer/stakeholders.md`.
2. Open the three files in `customer/data/` and skim them. Don't clean anything yet; just notice.
3. Follow **SKILLS.md → Customer discovery** and write at least 10 questions in `engagement/discovery-notes.md`.
4. Since there is no real customer, write the answer you *think* they'd give and mark it `ASSUMPTION`.
5. Fill in `engagement/scope.md`: problem, success metric, in/out of scope, risks.

## Things to notice
- Dana asked for "an API", but what Sam actually needs is to *know before the customer calls*.
- "Mostly clean" data is never clean.
- Lee (IT) said no data leaves AWS "except to approved vendors". Is an LLM provider approved? Ask now, not in week 5.

## Done when
- [ ] `scope.md` has one measurable success metric (e.g. "Sam sees late shipments within 1 hour").
- [ ] At least 3 risks listed, each with an owner.
- [ ] You can explain the project in two sentences to someone outside the company.
